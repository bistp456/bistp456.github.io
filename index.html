About

A multidisciplinary scientific perspective

Dr. Pradeep Bist earned his M.Sc., M.Phil., and Ph.D. in Microbiology in India. His doctoral research examined recombinant proteins expressed in Escherichia coli for serotyping foot-and-mouth disease virus.

His research career has encompassed virology, biochemistry, molecular biology, bacteriology, drug discovery, cancer biology, and inflammation. Working across these disciplines has shaped his interest in the connections between molecular mechanisms, cellular responses, host–pathogen interactions, and disease outcomes.

He currently serves as Senior Scientific Advisor at Celma Diagnostic Lab, Devaki Clinic, Hosur, Tamil Nadu. His present interests focus on virus–host interactions and emerging infectious diseases, particularly the roles of host responses in viral infection, tissue injury, and therapeutic intervention.

Scientific journey

Research across institutions and disciplines

Over the course of his career, Dr. Bist has worked in different scientific capacities at research institutions in India, the United States, and Singapore.

Moving between fields has offered opportunities to learn different experimental approaches and to consider how discoveries in one area of biology may illuminate questions in another.

His academic experience includes the Indian Veterinary Research Institute, Indian Institute of Science, University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), A*STAR, National University of Singapore, and Duke-NUS Medical School.
